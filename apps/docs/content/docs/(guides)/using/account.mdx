---
title: Your account
description: Profile, password, notifications, access tokens and AI assistants.
---

**Account** holds everything that is yours rather than a team's:

- **Profile and password.** Change your name, avatar and password. There is no reset email yet; a superadmin can set
  a temporary password (see [Users and teams](/docs/administration/users-and-teams)).
- **Notifications.** Let the browser tell you when a run starts or finishes.
- **Access tokens.** Personal access tokens (`pwr_pat_…`) for the [REST API](/docs/api) and the
  [MCP server](/docs/ai-assistants). Read-only, always expiring, and optionally limited to one team or one project.
  A token is shown once.
- **AI assistants.** Step-by-step setup for Claude, Cursor, VS Code, ChatGPT and others, pre-filled with your
  instance's URL, and a connection test.
- **Connected apps.** Assistants you signed in with OAuth, each of which can be disconnected.
- **Sessions.** Where you are signed in.
